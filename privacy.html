Cami Colorea Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · Leer en español

In short: Cami Colorea collects no data about the child or the adult with them. Drawings, stickers and settings stay stored inside the phone. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no servers of ours, and we never ask for a name, email, exact age, photos, voice or location.

What the app stores, and where

The app stores, only in the phone's own storage:

  • The drawings the child has coloured, exactly as they left them.
  • The stickers earned and where they have been placed.
  • Which drawings are finished and which are still locked.
  • The settings: the chosen age level, the language, and whether sound is on.

None of it leaves the device. Neither we nor anyone else can reach it.

What we do NOT do

  • We never ask for a name, email, date of birth or any personal detail.
  • There are no user accounts, no registration and no sign-in.
  • We do not use the camera, the microphone or location. The app does not request those permissions.
  • There is no chat, no comments and no way for the child to contact anyone.
  • There are no social media or external links inside the child's area.
  • We do not build advertising profiles of the child and we do not show personalised ads.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties, because we do not hold any.

The age level

At the start you choose a drawing level ("Little ones" or "Older"). It is a preference used to show suitable drawings, it is saved only on the phone, and it is not a date of birth and is never used to identify anyone.

The parents' area

Settings, purchases and any link leaving the app sit behind an adult gate: a sum that has to be solved to get in. The child cannot change the configuration or buy anything on their own.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, configured in child-directed mode. As required by Google, this means:

  • Ads are not personalised: they are not based on the interests or behaviour of whoever uses the app.
  • The device advertising identifier is not used to build profiles.
  • Ads go through Google's child-appropriate content filter.

To serve an ad, Google receives technical information about the device (such as country, language, device type and a signal that the ad was shown). Google processes that data as controller, under its own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Ads can be removed for good with a one-time purchase from the parents' area. After that, the app stops loading advertising.

In-app purchases

The one-time payment is handled by Google Play (or the App Store on iPhone). We never see or store card details. The store only tells us whether the purchase is active on that device. The purchase sits behind the parents' area, so an adult authorises it.

Printing and sharing drawings

The app can print a finished drawing or save it to send to the family. Both actions are always started by an adult from the parents' area and open the phone's own dialog: the operating system handles it, and the adult chooses the printer or the destination app.

The app never sends the drawing anywhere on its own and keeps no copy outside the phone. Once a drawing is passed to another service (email, messaging or any cloud), that service's terms apply, not ours.

Children

Cami Colorea is made for children and has been designed to meet Google Play's Designed for Families programme, Apple's Kids Category, the US COPPA rule and the European GDPR as it applies to minors.

Because no personal data is collected from anyone, no parental consent is needed for any processing: there simply is no processing of the child's data.

How to delete everything

Uninstalling the app erases everything it stored on the phone: drawings, stickers and settings. It is immediate and final. There is nothing to ask us for, because we hold no copy.

Changes to this policy

If the app changed and began handling data differently, we would update this page and say so inside the app before it happened.
